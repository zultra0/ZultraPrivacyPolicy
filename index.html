Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to the Zultra ecosystem. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our suite of applications, including Zultra Cloud, Zultra AI, Zultra Media, Zultra Notes, Zultra Drive, and Zultra Flow.

1. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

Our ecosystem utilizes Google Firebase for backend infrastructure and security. When using Zultra AI, the text prompts you enter are processed by Google's Gemini API. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy regarding how they handle data processed through their API endpoints.

2. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including Firebase Security Rules, to ensure that your data can only be accessed by your authenticated Zultra ID. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

3. Your Data Rights

You maintain full ownership of all data uploaded to the Zultra ecosystem. You reserve the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting our support channels.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new apps to the Zultra ecosystem. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
